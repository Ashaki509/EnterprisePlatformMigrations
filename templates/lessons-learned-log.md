## Writing a Useful Lesson

A lesson should change what someone does next time. Write it so a team that was not there can apply it.

| Weak | Useful |
|------|--------|
| Communication could have been better. | Users learned about the window two days ahead and missed it. Send the first notice [X] days ahead and repeat it the day before. |
| Testing took longer than expected. | Data validation took three times the estimate because the scripts ran in sequence. Run validation in parallel and time it in rehearsal. |

Record what went well in the same log. A practice worth repeating is a lesson too.

---

## Running the Session

Hold a 60-minute session after the pilot, after each wave, and at close.

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 5 min | Purpose and ground rules: focus on the process, not on individuals |
| 15 min | Each person writes what went well and what did not, individually |
| 20 min | Group the notes into themes and discuss the largest ones |
| 15 min | Agree on the lesson and an action for each theme |
| 5 min | Assign owners and dates |

---

## Turning Lessons Into Changes

- Every lesson with an action gets an owner and a due date.
- Review open actions at the next core team sync.
- Before each wave, read the lessons from the previous one and confirm the changes were made.
- At close, publish a summary for future migration teams.
